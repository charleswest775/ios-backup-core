== 1905-05-25
21:00 me: Where's your thesis? I've been waiting for months.
21:02 habicht: You'll get it when you send me something.
21:10 me: Fine. I can promise you four papers. The first is about radiation and the energy of light, and it is very revolutionary.
21:12 me: The second works out the real size of atoms from diffusion and viscosity.
21:14 me: The third shows that tiny particles in a liquid must jiggle because of the molecules around them. You can see it under a microscope.
21:16 me: The fourth is still a rough draft: the electrodynamics of moving bodies, which changes the theory of space and time.
21:20 habicht: Albert. That isn't four papers, it's a revolution.
