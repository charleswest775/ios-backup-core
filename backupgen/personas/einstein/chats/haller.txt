# Friedrich Haller, founding director of the Federal Office for Intellectual Property (the Patent Office).

== 1902-06-16
11:00 haller: Herr Einstein, the Federal Council has appointed you Technical Expert, third class, at 3,500 francs a year. Please report on 23 June.
11:15 me: Thank you, Herr Direktor. I will be there.

== 1902-06-23
08:00 me: Reporting for duty, Herr Direktor.

== 1904-09-16
10:30 haller: Your appointment is made permanent, with a salary of 3,900 francs.
10:40 me: Thank you, Herr Direktor.

== 1906-03-10
11:00 haller: You are promoted to Technical Expert, second class, from 1 April. Salary 4,500 francs.
11:05 me: Thank you. I will try to deserve it.

== 1909-07-06
16:00 me: Herr Direktor, I submit my resignation. I have been appointed to the University of Zurich.
16:30 haller: Accepted, with regret. Your last day will be 15 October.

== 1909-10-15
17:00 haller: Thank you for your service to the Office, Professor Einstein. Good luck in Zurich.
