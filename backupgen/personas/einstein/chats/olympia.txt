# The study circle of Einstein, Maurice Solovine and Conrad Habicht. Sources date its start to 1902 or to Easter 1903;
# the three were close friends by January 1903 (both were wedding witnesses). Supper was a sausage, Gruyère, fruit,
# honey and tea. The caviar and hard-boiled-egg stories are from Solovine's recollections; their exact dates are unknown.

== 1902-04-12
19:30 solovine: Tonight at Albert's? I've finished the first chapters of Pearson's Grammar of Science.
19:34 habicht: I'll bring the sausage.
19:35 me: I'll bring the tea. And the questions.

== 1902-04-19
23:55 habicht: It's nearly midnight and we've only reached chapter three.
23:56 me: That's because chapter three is wrong.
23:56 solovine +laugh

== 1902-05-10
18:20 me: Next book: Mach's Mechanics. He has no patience for absolute space, and neither do I.
18:31 solovine: Agreed. Absolute space has never once been observed.

== 1902-06-24
20:15 me: Patent Office, day two. My colleagues are serious men. I've stopped humming.
20:20 habicht: Hum at the Academy instead.

== 1902-11-29
21:40 solovine: Hume's Treatise next week. He'll ruin cause and effect for you, Albert.
21:45 me: Good. I never trusted them.

== 1902-12-11
20:05 habicht: Lorentz and Zeeman have the Nobel Prize in physics.
20:12 me: For the splitting of spectral lines in a magnetic field. Lorentz deserves a dozen.

== 1903-01-07
10:00 me: Thank you both for standing with us yesterday. And for not mentioning the key.
10:03 habicht: We will mention the key for the rest of your life.
10:03 solovine +laugh

== 1903-01-31
19:00 me: The Academy has decided I'm its president. I accept, as Albert Ritter von Steissbein.
19:01 solovine: Sir Albert of Tailbone. It suits you.
19:02 habicht: All hail Steissbein.

== 1903-03-14
23:10 solovine: Did you enjoy your birthday supper, Mr President?
23:11 me: Very much. Was there anything special in it?
23:12 habicht: It was caviar, Albert. You ate all of it while explaining Galileo's principle of inertia.
23:13 me: Then I've learned my lesson: caviar is wasted on me.
23:13 solovine +laugh

== 1903-04-18
18:00 solovine: Poincaré's Science and Hypothesis. We need a whole week for it.
18:05 me: We need a whole month. It's the best book I've read this year.
18:06 habicht +like

== 1903-06-20
22:30 habicht: Spinoza tonight or Don Quixote?
22:31 me: Don Quixote. Tilting at windmills is excellent preparation for physics.

== 1903-09-26
20:00 solovine: I'm not coming tonight. There's a concert. Hard-boiled eggs on the table for you both, with my apologies.
23:45 habicht: We ate the eggs. Your room is now upside down and full of smoke. Steissbein says good night.
23:46 solovine: You are both barbarians.
23:46 me +laugh

== 1903-12-18
21:00 me: Café Bollwerk after the Office tomorrow? Mill's Logic is too long for one evening.
21:05 solovine: The Bollwerk, then. Bring your pipe.
21:06 habicht: I'll bring cigars.
21:06 me: Habicht's cigars are why we sit by the door.

== 1904-01-09
19:45 habicht: Did you read that the American brothers flew a machine with an engine?
19:50 me: Twelve seconds. Pearson would say we need more observations.

== 1904-02-10
08:30 solovine: Japan has attacked the Russian fleet at Port Arthur. It's war.
08:40 me: Another war. I gave up my German citizenship partly to stay out of uniforms.

# Habicht left Bern in 1904; exact month unknown.

== 1904-08-20
18:30 habicht: My last Academy evening in Bern for a while. A teaching post awaits.
18:31 me: The Academy will hold your chair. And your sausage.
18:32 solovine +love

== 1905-03-14
21:00 solovine: Happy birthday, Mr President. Twenty-six!
21:05 me: Thank you. I'm about to send the Annalen a paper you'll both enjoy.


== 1905-06-10
20:00 solovine: Read your light-quanta paper. I understood the first page and admired the rest.
20:05 me: That's the right ratio.
20:06 habicht: I understood the title.
20:06 me +laugh

== 1905-09-28
21:00 solovine: Besso says you have a new paper where clocks disagree. Explain it at supper on Saturday?
21:05 me: Bring an extra cervelat. It'll take a while.

# Solovine left Bern for Lyon in November 1905.
== 1905-11-15
20:00 solovine: I'm off to Lyon. The Academy is now a correspondence course.
20:05 me: An academy of two in different cities is still an academy.
20:06 habicht: Three. Don't forget me in exile.
20:06 solovine +love
