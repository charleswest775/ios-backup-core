Crater Lake, Sep 20 to 21
Who: me, Theo, Leah, Callum, Nadia, Sam (Tom in Iceland)
Sat
- Leave 6am, 4.5 hrs
- Cleetwood Cove trail down to the dock by 12:15. It's steep, ~700 ft over a mile
- Boat 12:45, ~3 hrs on Wizard Island. Hike to the summit cone, swim at Fumarole Bay if brave
- Hike back up Cleetwood (slowly)
- Camp at Mazama
Sun
- Rim Drive clockwise. Phantom Ship overlook, Pinnacles, Watchman Peak trail for the view
- Home by dinner
