Linden kids' workshop
22 kids from the summer program, ages 6 to 11. What they drew, most to least:

- A slide down the hill
- Water to splash in, "a frog pond"
- Big rocks to climb on
- Shade, a tree to sit under
- A "quiet tree for when you're sad or reading"
- A tunnel
- Somewhere to grow strawberries
Grace: teachers want an outdoor classroom that holds a full class of 28
