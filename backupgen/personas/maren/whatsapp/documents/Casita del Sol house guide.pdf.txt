Casita del Sol: house guide
Welcome to Joshua Tree! A few things to know.
Check-in is after 3 pm and check-out is by 11 am. The key is in the lockbox by the front door.
The Wi-Fi network and password are on the fridge.
Please keep the gate closed. The desert tortoises are slow but curious.
Quiet hours are 10 pm to 7 am. The stars are the main attraction: the hammock and the fire pit are yours.
Trash and recycling go in the bins by the carport. Please don't leave food outside.
Nearest groceries: the market on Highway 62, five minutes by car.
Questions? Message Dani on WhatsApp.
