# Maren Lindqvist: storyline

Entirely fictional. Written for backupgen and released CC0-1.0. Phone numbers are in the
US 555-0100 to 0199 fictional range; emails use example.com/.org/.net. Real places, parks and
public agencies are named; every person, business, school and the firm are invented.

All times are Pacific (America/Los_Angeles), even when she is travelling.

## Who's who

**Maren Lindqvist** (owner, `maren`). Born 22 Oct 1992, so 31 when the period opens and 33 at
the end. Landscape architect at Tanager Landscape Studio, a six-person firm on Portland's
Central Eastside. MLA from the University of Oregon (2018). Grew up in Missoula, Montana.
Commutes by bike, climbs Tuesday nights, hikes most weekends. Dog: **Juniper** ("June",
"Junie"), a four-year-old border collie / Aussie mix. Car: an old green Subaru ("the
Forester").

**Home.** Until April 2025 she and Theo rent a second-floor one-bedroom in SE Portland ("the
apartment"). On Sat 12 Apr 2025 they move into the half of a duplex in NE with a fenced yard
("the duplex", "the new place"), rented from Gloria Pham. Next door: Bev Sorensen.

**Partner.** Theo Navarro (`theo`), together about five years. Seventh-grade science teacher at
Kestrel Middle School. Bakes sourdough (the starter is called Gus), fixes things, terrible
at packing light. Grew up in Bend; his mom is Lucía Navarro (`lucia`), who texts Maren in
Spanish. Birthday 3 June.

**Best friend.** Leah Brandt (`leah`), friends since grad school in Eugene. Hydrologist at
Clearwater Environmental. Lives in Sellwood with her cat, Pickles. Birthday 9 Nov. Starts
seeing Callum Reid (`callum`) in early summer 2025; he joins trips from autumn 2025.

**Hiking group, "Switchbacks & Snacks"** (`hiking` group chat): Maren, Theo, Leah, Sam Okafor
(`sam`, owns the big car, drives most carpools), Nadia Haddad (`nadia`, librarian, permit and
spreadsheet wizard), Tom Becker (`tom`, photographer in Vancouver WA, always ten minutes
late, always brings the good snacks).

**Climbing.** Basalt Climbing Gym, Tuesday nights. Ivy Chen (`ivy`, illustrator, climbing
partner since 2023), Jonah Pierce (`jonah`, route setter at Basalt), Rosa Delgado (`rosa`,
structural engineer, moved from Albuquerque in Jan 2025; Maren meets her at the gym on Sun
9 Feb 2025). Maren and Rosa trade Spanish/English practice by text. Group chat "Tuesday
Crushers" (`climbing`) from March 2025.

**Family** (group chat "Lindqvists 🏔" = `family`). Mom Karin (`mom`, retired music teacher,
still teaches piano), Dad Greg (`dad`, semi-retired land surveyor, loves maps), both in
Missoula. Brother Erik (`erik`, born 4 Dec 1995, head roaster at Basin Coffee Roasters in Boise; partner
Jess Moreno, `jess`). Grandma Ingrid (`ingrid`, Missoula) and Aunt Linnea Berg (`linnea`,
Seattle) call on birthdays. Birthdays: Grandma 2 Feb, Mom 30 Mar, Dad 19 Jul, Erik 4 Dec.

**Work, Tanager Landscape Studio** (`tanager` main line). Priya Raman (`priya`, founding
principal), Owen Castellanos (`owen`, senior LA, runs Cottonwood Landing), Bea Thornton
(`bea`, studio manager), Kenji Mori (`kenji`, designer, starts Mon 3 Mar 2025). Group chat
"Cottonwood crew" (`cottonwood`): Owen, Kenji, Priya.

**Projects.**
- *Linden Elementary schoolyard redesign* (fictional school). Client contact: principal Grace
  Adeyemi (`grace`). Depaving most of the asphalt, a nature-play hill with a slide, a rain
  garden, a log-circle outdoor classroom, native planting. Kids' design workshop Wed 17 Jul
  2024; construction documents due Thu 31 Oct 2024; bids in Jan 2025; construction 16 Jun to
  29 Aug 2025 by Hollis Grade & Build (superintendent Ray Duarte, `ray`); ribbon cutting Sat
  6 Sep 2025.
- *Cottonwood Landing riverfront park* on the Willamette for the (fictional) Riverbend Parks
  Foundation, contact Natalie Cho (`natalie`). Floodable terraces, cottonwood and willow
  restoration, a kayak launch, a boardwalk. Kickoff Jan 2025; community open house Sat 22 Feb
  2025; design development presentation Tue 18 Nov 2025; permit submittal Fri 20 Mar 2026.
- Maren is promoted to Associate on Mon 12 Jan 2026.

**Others.** Ashley Kim (`ashley`, neighbor at the old apartment), Hana Yoshida (`hana`, grad-
school friend in Seattle), Claire Dubois (`claire`, grad-school friend in Eugene), Maya Ruiz
(`maya`, dog sitter, from Jul 2025), Doug Harlan (`doug`, cabin host in Government Camp).

**Businesses (all fictional).** Alderbrook Veterinary Clinic (Juniper's vet), Crank & Coast
Cycles, Mossgrove Family Dental, Thimbleberry Native Nursery, Basalt Climbing Gym, Stonecrop
Property Management (old apartment), Tidewater Moving Co., Ridgeline Auto Repair, Fernleaf
Salon, Cedar & Salt Pizza, Talus Outdoor Supply (rentals), Sudsy Pup Grooming, Willow & Ash
Frame Shop.
